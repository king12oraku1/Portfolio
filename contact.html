Frequently Asked

Common questions about working with me.

What is your typical project timeline?
Most projects take 1-3 weeks depending on complexity. Simple landing pages can be ready in 3-5 days, while larger websites may take 2-4 weeks. I'll provide a detailed timeline during our initial consultation.
Do you provide website maintenance?
Yes! I offer monthly maintenance packages that include security updates, content changes, performance monitoring, and technical support to keep your website running smoothly.
What technologies do you specialize in?
I specialize in HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, and Bootstrap 5. I also work with jQuery, SASS, and various CSS animation libraries to create dynamic, interactive experiences.
How do we get started?
Simply fill out the contact form or send me an email. We'll schedule a free consultation to discuss your requirements, timeline, and budget before I provide a detailed proposal.
Do you offer payment plans?
Yes, I typically work with a 50% upfront deposit and 50% upon completion. For larger projects, we can arrange milestone-based payments to make it more manageable.
